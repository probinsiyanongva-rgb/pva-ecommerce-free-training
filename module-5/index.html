Inventory Basics

Inventory is what makes every promise on a listing true or false. This module teaches store-level inventory monitoring -- reading stock data, catching problems early, and knowing when to escalate.

You do it at Everfield's operations desk on a Wednesday: this morning's stock sheet, ClearPath's cycle count and the day's movements. Read, flag, investigate, record.

Scope note: This module covers store-level inventory monitoring only. Deeper warehouse operations, replenishment planning, and supplier-side inventory work belong to a separate, more advanced logistics specialization.
