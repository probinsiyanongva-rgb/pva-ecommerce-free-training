Returns, Refunds & Exceptions

Most orders go fine. This module is about the ones that don't -- building a calm, consistent way to handle cancellations, returns, damaged items, and missing packages without guessing.

Here you do it at Everfield's operations desk: cases come in from Sofia and Maya, you inspect the records and Everfield's policy, make the call, and document it so someone else can act.

Reminder: Exception handling is documentation first, decisions second. A beginner E-commerce VA records what actually happened and follows policy -- and escalates the moment a case falls outside it. Everfield's policy is a simulation training pattern; real clients write their own.
