Products, SKUs & Product Data

Every task you'll do later -- listings, orders, inventory -- rests on product data being organized correctly. This module teaches you how Everfield structures its catalog, and how to keep that structure clean.

You do it at Everfield's product desk on a Tuesday: references to match to the right SKU, a carrier charge to trace back to its field, and two master-sheet rows to finish. Every value comes from a source, or it gets flagged.

Reminder: Everfield's SKUs, categories, and product fields are simulation details built for this training. Real clients structure their catalogs differently -- what matters is recognizing the pattern.
