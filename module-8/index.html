E-commerce Research

Good research is disciplined, not clever -- consistent formats, verified sources, and a clear line between what you observed and what you'd recommend. This module builds exactly that discipline.

You do it at Everfield's research desk on a Friday: questions to sort before anyone searches, outside listings to compare fairly, and a report for Sofia. Every outside store, price and review on this desk is simulated training data.

Scope note: This module covers useful, basic research habits -- not advanced competitive intelligence or specialized market analysis. Those sit outside this Foundation. The outside stores, listings, prices and reviews you'll work with are fictional training data, not real or current market information.
