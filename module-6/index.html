Customer Support in E-commerce

Support questions are really questions about the systems you've already learned -- orders, products, inventory. This module teaches you to answer accurately from real data, and to know your own limits.

Reminder: A beginner E-commerce VA answers from real information and documented policy -- never by guessing. When something falls outside policy or your authority, the right move is to escalate, not improvise.
