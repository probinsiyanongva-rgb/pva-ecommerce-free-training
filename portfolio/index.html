Your beginner E-commerce VA portfolio

Certain lessons across this course have an Apply step -- that's where you build a real work sample, in your own words, grounded in Everfield Goods. Together, they build toward a 13-piece beginner portfolio, finishing with the Final Portfolio you assemble in the Capstone.

Portfolio note: these are fictional training work samples created through Probinsiyanong VA Free Training. They demonstrate practiced skills -- they should not be presented as paid client work, client results, or professional experience that did not occur. Save your finished work samples yourself as you go; this site does not store your written answers.
